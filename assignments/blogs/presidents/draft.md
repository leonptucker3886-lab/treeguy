# Presidents Ranked by the Numbers

*J-1001 draft by Treeguy. Pending Inspector gadget review. Not live on leonlink.net.*

How do you rank U.S. presidents with data instead of vibes? There's no single scoreboard, so this post combines two kinds of evidence: **expert surveys** (historians and political scientists scoring every president) and **hard statistics** from each term (economy, approval).

## 1. What the experts say

Two large, repeated surveys give the most consistent ranking:

| Rank | C-SPAN 2021 Historians Survey | Siena College 2022 Survey |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Abraham Lincoln | Franklin D. Roosevelt |
| 2 | George Washington | Abraham Lincoln |
| 3 | Franklin D. Roosevelt | George Washington |
| 4 | Theodore Roosevelt | Theodore Roosevelt |
| 5 | Dwight D. Eisenhower | Thomas Jefferson |

The same four names (Lincoln, Washington, FDR, Theodore Roosevelt) hold the top spots in both. At the bottom, both surveys place James Buchanan, Andrew Johnson and Franklin Pierce among the lowest.

C-SPAN scores 10 categories, including crisis leadership, economic management, public persuasion and pursuit of equal justice. Siena scores 20 attributes such as background, integrity, intelligence and accomplishments.

## 2. What the term statistics say

**Gallup approval (average across the term, modern era):** John F. Kennedy had the highest average approval of any president Gallup has measured (about 70%), followed by Eisenhower (about 65%). Harry Truman's average was among the lowest (about 45%), even though historians now rank him in the top ten. Popularity in office and historical rank don't always match.

**Economy:** Real GDP growth and job creation vary widely by term, but economists caution that presidents get too much credit and blame: the business cycle, the Federal Reserve and Congress all matter. Treat economic tables as context, not a verdict.

## 3. A combined view

Taking the average of the two expert surveys gives this data-backed top five:

1. **Abraham Lincoln** (avg rank 1.5)
2. **Franklin D. Roosevelt** (avg 2.0)
3. **George Washington** (avg 2.5)
4. **Theodore Roosevelt** (avg 4.0)
5. **Thomas Jefferson / Dwight D. Eisenhower** (close behind)

## 4. Caveats
- Surveys measure expert opinion, which shifts over time (Eisenhower and Grant have both risen in recent decades).
- Approval data only exists from the 1930s onward.
- Recent presidents are usually not ranked until history has more distance.
